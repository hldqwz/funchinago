The best China travel apps are not the same for every traveler. A better way to think is by task: how will you pay, translate, navigate, book trains, contact hotels, and get help when something fails?

Install the critical apps before you fly. Some setup steps are harder after arrival.

## Quick Answer

Prepare apps for payment, translation, maps, messaging, train tickets, booking, and ride-hailing. At minimum, first-time visitors should prepare [Alipay](/china-travel/articles/how-to-use-alipay-in-china-foreigner/), [WeChat Pay](/china-travel/articles/how-to-use-wechat-pay-in-china-foreigner/), a translation app, a map option, hotel booking access, and a train-ticket plan if traveling between cities.

## Payment Apps

Payment is the first category to solve.

Prepare:

- [Alipay as your first payment app](/china-travel/articles/how-to-use-alipay-in-china-foreigner/).
- [WeChat / WeChat Pay as backup and communication layer](/china-travel/articles/how-to-use-wechat-pay-in-china-foreigner/).
- Your bank app for fraud alerts.
- A backup card or cash plan.

Do not assume international credit cards will work everywhere. Daily China travel often moves through QR payments.

If payment is your main worry, start with [How to Pay in China as a Tourist](/china-travel/articles/how-to-pay-in-china-tourist/) before choosing the rest of your app stack.

## Translation Apps

A translation app is essential for restaurants, taxis, stations, and small shops.

Before arrival:

- Download offline language packs if available.
- Test camera translation.
- Save common phrases.
- Prepare allergy or dietary restriction phrases.

Offline support matters because your first problem may happen before your data plan is working.

## Maps And Navigation

Map choice can be tricky. Some foreign map services may have limitations in mainland China, while local map apps can be powerful but harder for non-Chinese speakers.

Prepare at least one map option you can actually use. Save your hotel address in Chinese and English. Screenshot your first airport-to-hotel route.

## Train And Transport Apps

If you will take high-speed rail, check the official 12306 English site and decide whether you want to book directly or through a travel platform.

For city transport, payment apps and mini programs may also help with metro, taxi, or ride-hailing access depending on the city and your account setup.

## Messaging And Hotel Contact

WeChat can be useful because many hotels, guides, restaurants, or local services may use it for communication. Even if you do not use it for payment, it can still be useful as a contact tool.

## Food And Attraction Apps

Many restaurants and attractions use QR codes, mini programs, or local booking flows. This is where WeChat and Alipay can become more than payment apps.

For a first trip, do not try to master every local app. Prepare the basics and use hotel staff or travel platforms when a local-only process is too hard.

## What To Set Up Before Landing

Before departure, prepare:

- Payment login and card setup.
- Translation offline data.
- Hotel booking access.
- First hotel address in Chinese.
- Mobile data or eSIM.
- Train account or booking platform.
- Bank app access.
