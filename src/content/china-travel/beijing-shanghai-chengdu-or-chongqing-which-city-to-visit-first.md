If this is your first trip to China, your first city matters. Some cities are better for history, some for food, some for easy logistics, and some for visual drama.

## Quick Answer

- Beijing for history and classic landmarks.
- Shanghai for the easiest first landing.
- Chengdu for food and a slower pace.
- Chongqing for dramatic city visuals and a strong wow factor.

## Beijing

Best for:

- History.
- Museums.
- Imperial landmarks.
- A classic first look at China.

Why choose it:

- It gives you the clearest "China" first trip experience.
- It connects well to Xi'an and Shanghai.

Watch out for:

- Large distances between attractions.
- A trip that becomes tiring if you overpack the day.

## Shanghai

Best for:

- A smooth first landing.
- Easy transport.
- Walkable city zones.
- Modern city life.

Why choose it:

- It is often the most comfortable first city for foreign visitors.
- It is a good place to learn how payment, metro, and hotel flows work in China.

Watch out for:

- It can feel less "historic" than travelers expect.

## Chengdu

Best for:

- Food.
- Pandas.
- Tea house culture.
- A slower pace.

Why choose it:

- It feels relaxed without being boring.
- It is a strong food city for first-time visitors.
- It now has a dedicated [Chengdu first-time visitor guide](/china-travel/articles/chengdu-travel-guide-first-time-visitors/) if you want pandas, hotpot, tea houses, and a slower route.

Watch out for:

- If you are very spice-sensitive, plan your meals carefully.

## Chongqing

Best for:

- City visuals.
- Hotpot.
- Night views.
- A more dramatic travel story.

Why choose it:

- It feels unlike most Western cities.
- It is memorable even if you only stay a few days.

Watch out for:

- Vertical geography can make navigation more confusing.
- It can feel intense as a first and only China stop.

## My Simple Recommendation

If you want the safest first city, start with Shanghai.

If you want the strongest classic route, start with Beijing.

If you want the most enjoyable food-first trip, start with Chengdu.

If you want the strongest visual surprise, add Chongqing after one easier city.
