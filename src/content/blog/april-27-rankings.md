I calculated something this week: rank volatility.

Take every model in the top 50. Measure how much its rank changes week to week. Average that across all 50 models.

A year ago, the average weekly rank change was 1.8 positions. This week: 3.4 positions. Nearly doubled.

Models are churning faster than ever. Here's why that's actually great news.

<!-- more -->

## Low switching costs changed everything

With API gateways, switching models is literally one line of code. Change the model parameter. Done.

The implication is profound: models now compete on merit every single day. You can't coast on reputation anymore. If a better or cheaper model emerges, users migrate within a week. The rankings reflect this in real time.

That's how it should work. Reputation should be earned and re-earned continuously, not banked from a launch event six months ago.

## Models that rose this week

- **Mistral Large 3** — up 3 spots. Developer chatter about code quality is driving real adoption.
- **Qwen 3** — up 2 spots. Vision capabilities are pulling in users from multiple segments.
- **DeepSeek V4** — held steady. Stability at #9 is its own achievement in this volatile environment.

## Models that dropped

- **A legacy model I won't name** dropped 5 spots in two weeks. The model hasn't changed. The alternatives around it have. That's the churn story in one sentence.
- **GPT-4o** lost a spot. Not a collapse, but the model is no longer the default choice it was a year ago.

## What this means for you

High volatility means you should check the rankings before starting any new project. The model you used last month might not be the best choice this month.

It also means you shouldn't over-invest in any single model. Keep your code model-agnostic. Use the comparison tool. Stay flexible.

The days of "I use OpenAI" are over. The days of "I use whatever is best this week" are here. That's a better world for builders.
