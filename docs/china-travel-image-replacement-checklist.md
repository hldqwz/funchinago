# China Starter Portal Image Replacement Checklist

Updated: 2026-06-04

## Goal

Make the site feel more trustworthy, more Western-editorial, and less AI-generated by replacing the most visible synthetic-looking images first.

Rule of thumb:

- Homepage and first-level landing pages should look photo-led.
- Real photos should win in city, food, street-life, and culture contexts.
- AI images can stay longer in abstract planning, checklist, transit, and app-explainer contexts.
- If accuracy or realism affects trust, prioritize real photography.

## Priority System

- `P0` = replace first, highly visible and trust-shaping
- `P1` = replace next, important but not blocking first impression
- `P2` = can keep for now, acceptable as transitional assets

## Practical Sourcing Guidance

Preferred order:

1. Real photos you are confident we can legally use
2. Reusable licensed image sources
3. Your own collected editorial-style assets
4. AI-generated fallback only where realism is less important

Avoid as default:

- random scraped Xiaohongshu or Douyin images directly copied into the site
- heavily watermarked images
- images with unclear usage rights
- images that look like tourism posters rather than editorial travel photography

## My Strong Recommendation

If we only do one image task next, do this:

- replace the homepage hero
- replace the 3 Start Here cards
- replace the 4 city cards

That single batch will change the whole site's perceived quality much more than replacing ten lower-traffic article covers.
