## Ranking-band interpretation

- Average position 1–10: prioritize CTR and snippet alignment. This applies most clearly to the Apple Pay / Google Pay page (7.3) and the Alipay vs WeChat Pay comparison (8.7).
- Average position 11–20: observe both CTR and ranking movement. The China Travel homepage is close to this band at 13.6.
- Average position beyond 20: prioritize impression growth, query ownership, internal links, and ranking improvement before judging content by clicks. This applies to Payment Hub, Alipay, WeChat Pay, Travel, and the Checklist.
